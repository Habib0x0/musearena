01 / Designer

Conversational, but schema-gated.

Describe the organization, its systems, and the weakness the exercise should make provable. The designer produces a canonical EnvironmentSpec, a topology graph, a per-host build sheet, and a cost estimate.

  • Live streaming of the agent's reasoning, tool calls, and repair attempts
  • Catalog-derived service vocabulary, so designs stay deployable
  • Budget gate with the exact violated cap named
  • Refinement, forking, and JSON import/export

02 / Deployment

Real guests, real verification.

Pulumi provisions local or managed KVM/QEMU guests. Configuration is per-instance, and readiness is proven from one guest against another — not from the host's own logs.

  • FreeIPA with Kerberos and GSSAPI keytabs
  • auditd and osquery sensors required before scoring
  • Cross-host probes for DNS, LDAP, SMTP, HTTP, and SQL
  • TTL-based teardown with no leaked processes

03 / Exercise

Scored on evidence, not activity.

Red and blue runs are scored against declared objectives, detection coverage, and false-positive baselines. A blind sensor fails the run instead of producing a meaningless score.

  • Objectives with verifiable success signals
  • Time-to-detect and containment metrics
  • Benign control runs establish the FP baseline
  • Live drift reporting when a guest is changed out of band

04 / Export

Traces you can re-verify.

Every run exports a content-hashed dataset with MITRE ATT&CK labels and version-pinned tooling. Re-download it, check the hash, and replay the score offline.

  • Verifiable spans for every model call
  • Hash-chained ground truth
  • Redaction levels for sharing
  • Offline replay reproduces the score
