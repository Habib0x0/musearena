The loop

From a brief to evidence, in four checked steps.

Every design passes the same checks a careful reviewer would apply before anything runs.

01 / Design

Conversational designer

A clear specification, a topology map, and a cost check. Designs over budget are stopped with the exact limit explained.

02 / Deploy

Provision real guests

Local or managed virtual machines, configured per host and checked for exploitability.

03 / Exercise

Red and blue agents

Attacks and defenses scored on goals, detection time, and containment — not on log volume.

04 / Export

Verifiable traces

Datasets with content hashes and ATT&CK labels you can verify and replay.

Why teams use it

Built for proof, not demos.

◇

Provable objectives

Every goal has a measurable result. Designs without a provable outcome are rejected.

✓

Honest scoring

Readiness checks and false-positive baselines. A blind sensor fails the run instead of scoring it.
